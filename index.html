What is RPH Bijak Transfer?

RPH Bijak Transfer is a Windows application for teachers in Malaysia. It copies lesson content from lesson-plan (RPH) documents the teacher already owns into the teacher's own RPH template, and fills in the date, day, time, class and week from the teacher's timetable. The teacher reviews everything and downloads a Word (.docx) file.

  • Makes the RPH for a whole week, for every class, in one document.
  • Continues from the last lesson each week, and repeats lessons that could not be taught.
  • Keeps the template's own fonts, tables, logo and layout.
  • Runs on the teacher's own computer. Documents are not uploaded to us.

Apa itu RPH Bijak Transfer?

RPH Bijak Transfer ialah aplikasi Windows untuk guru di Malaysia. Aplikasi ini memindahkan isi RPH yang sudah dimiliki oleh guru ke dalam templat RPH guru sendiri, dan mengisi tarikh, hari, masa, kelas dan minggu mengikut jadual waktu. Guru semak dahulu, kemudian muat turun fail Word (.docx).

  • Jana RPH untuk seminggu, bagi semua kelas, dalam satu dokumen.
  • Sambung dari RPH terakhir setiap minggu, dan ulang RPH yang tidak sempat diajar.
  • Format templat anda (fon, jadual, logo) dikekalkan.
  • Berjalan dalam komputer anda sendiri. Dokumen tidak dimuat naik kepada kami.

How the app uses Google sign-in

Signing in with Google is used for two things only:

  • Licence: the app reads the e-mail address of the connected Google account to check that it matches the licence the teacher bought.
  • Google Drive (optional): the teacher can save finished RPH files and a backup of their setup into one folder, “RPH Bijak Transfer”, in their own Google Drive.

The app asks only for access to files it created itself. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the user's Google Drive. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
