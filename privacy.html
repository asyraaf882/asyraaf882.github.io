Last updated: 13 November 2026

RPH Bijak Transfer (“the app”) is made by AsyraafMJ, trading as Bijak Studio (“we”). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept and what choices you have.

Summary

  • The app runs on your own computer. Your documents and settings are stored on your computer.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your documents, and we do not use analytics or advertising.
  • If you connect a Google account, the app can only access files that the app itself created in your Google Drive.

1. Information kept on your computer

The app saves the following in a folder named my_data inside the app folder on your computer:

  • your RPH template, the lesson-plan files you add, your timetable and class names;
  • which lesson each class has reached;
  • your licence key, and a record of how much of the free trial has been used;
  • if you connect Google: the sign-in token issued by Google and the e-mail address of that account.

A small record of trial use is also kept in your Windows user profile. None of this is sent to us.

2. Google user data

Connecting a Google account is optional unless your licence is tied to a Google account.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google account e-mail address, to check that it matches the licence you bought and to show you which account is connected.
  • Files the app itself creates in your Google Drive (Google permission drive.file): finished RPH documents and a backup file of your setup, stored in a folder named “RPH Bijak Transfer”.

What the app does not access

The app cannot see, read, change or delete any other file in your Google Drive, and it does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

How Google data is used, stored and shared

  • It is used only to provide the features above, on your own computer.
  • The sign-in token and e-mail address are stored only on your computer. Files saved to Drive stay in your own Google Drive.
  • Google user data is not sent to us, not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to develop or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Your licence

When you buy, we ask for your name and the Google account e-mail address (Gmail or DELIMa) you will use with the app. We keep your name, that address, the order date and the expiry date in a private licence list that only we can open.

To check your licence, the app sends to that list: the e-mail address of the Google account signed in to the app, a short code for your computer (made from your Windows installation; it does not contain your name or files) and the app version. The list records the codes of the computers that use your licence (at most two) and when the app last checked. This happens about once a day when you are online.

If you were given a licence key instead, we keep a record of the key and your name, e-mail address or computer code. Payment is made through your bank or a payment provider; we do not see or store card details. We do not sell or share this information.

4. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your computer: everything stays until you delete it. Deleting the my_data folder removes your setup, documents and sign-in from the app.
  • Google connection: use “Log keluar Drive” in the app to remove the sign-in from your computer and ask Google to cancel the app's access. You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Files in your Drive: they are yours. Delete the “RPH Bijak Transfer” folder in Google Drive to remove them.
  • Your licence record with us: contact us and we will delete it. The licence key can then no longer be re-issued.

5. Security

The app is available only on your own computer (it does not accept connections from other computers). Communication with Google uses Google's encrypted connections. Anyone who can use your Windows account can open the app and its files, so protect your computer with a password.

6. Children

The app is a work tool for teachers. It is not intended for children and does not collect information about pupils. Lesson plans should not contain pupils' personal information.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
